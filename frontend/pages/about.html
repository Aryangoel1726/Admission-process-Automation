About AdmissionPro

AdmissionPro is a modern admission process automation system designed to eliminate the hassle of paper-based college admissions. Our platform enables students to apply, upload documents, and track their application status online — anytime, anywhere.


🎯 Our Mission

To make higher-education admissions transparent, efficient, and accessible to every student regardless of their location.


🛠️ Key Features

  • Online registration and application submission
  • Secure document upload and verification
  • Real-time application tracking dashboard
  • Automated merit list generation
  • Online fee payment integration
  • Email & SMS notifications at every step

📌 Project Status

This project is currently under active development. The student registration, login, and application submission modules are functional. Admin dashboard, document upload, merit list generation, and payment integration are coming soon.
